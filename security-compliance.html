Security & Compliance

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information Security Governance

Oriental Consultants operates strict digital and operational safeguards across all audit, accounting, and cloud infrastructure engagements. Our IT governance models align with ISO/IEC 27001 information security principles, ensuring client files, financial ledgers, and institutional data remain secure against unauthorized access, alteration, or interception.

2. Statutory & Regulatory Licensing in Afghanistan

We maintain full statutory accreditation in Afghanistan:

  • Commercial Registry: Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MoCI) License No. I-11011.
  • Tax Authority: Ministry of Finance General Directorate of Revenue (TIN: 1045181011).
  • International Network: Independent member of PrimeGlobal USA, a global association of independent advisory and accounting firms.
  • Global Identifier: Dun & Bradstreet D-U-N-S® Number: 850550597.

3. Financial Anti-Money Laundering (AML/CFT) Compliance

In accordance with Da Afghanistan Bank (Central Bank) regulations and FinTRACA guidelines, our forensic and assurance practitioners enforce rigorous Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering procedures. We perform fiduciary verification on all donor-funded and international grant engagements.

4. Cloud Infrastructure & Encryption Standards

For cloud hosting, QuikBok remote deployments, and TSplus setups:

  • Data in Transit: Encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with modern cipher suites.
  • Data at Rest: Encrypted backup storage with role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Authentication: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforced on all administrative portals and client systems.

5. Audit Working Paper Confidentiality

Under International Standards on Quality Management (ISQM 1), all client audit files, donor verifications, and tax clearance documentation are restricted to appointed audit engagement team members under strict non-disclosure obligations.

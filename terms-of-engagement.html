Terms of Engagement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Engagement Letter & Contractual Scope

These Terms of Engagement govern professional advisory, audit, tax compliance, software implementation, and corporate services rendered by Oriental Consultants Afghanistan to clients. Individual engagements are formalized through an executed Engagement Letter specifying agreed deliverables, timelines, and fee schedules.

2. Professional Standards & Quality Control

All assurance and statutory audit engagements are executed in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (ISA) issued by the IAASB and ethical codes issued by IESBA. Tax filings and clearances comply with the Afghanistan Income Tax Law and BRT regulations administered by the Ministry of Finance.

3. Client Responsibilities & Document Provision

The client agrees to provide accurate, complete, and timely records, vouchers, agreements, bank statements, and executive explanations required for the performance of our duties. Delays in document submission by the client may result in adjusted delivery timelines.

4. Invoicing, Fees & Retainers

Fees are determined on either a fixed project fee or milestone basis as established in the Engagement Letter. Invoices are payable upon presentation in USD or Afghan Afghanis (AFN) at the central bank conversion rate on invoice date.

5. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These terms and all engagement contracts are governed by and construed under the applicable laws and commercial codes of Afghanistan. Any disputes that cannot be settled amicably shall be submitted to mediation or arbitration in Kabul, Afghanistan.

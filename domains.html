Frequently Asked Questions

Domain Registration in Afghanistan FAQ

What documents are required to register a .af or .com.af domain?

For generic .af domains, registration is open to businesses, NGOs, and individuals worldwide. For .com.af, a valid business license (Jawaz) or national ID/Tazkira from Afghanistan is recommended. For .ngo and .org.af, an official NGO registration certificate from the Afghan Ministry of Economy is required.

How long does it take for a domain to become active?

Global domains (.com, .net, .org, .ai, .io) are activated in under 60 seconds. For Afghan ccTLDs (.af, .com.af, .ngo), registry verification typically completes within 2 to 4 hours during Kabul business hours.

Can I link my .af domain to Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?

Yes! All domains registered through Oriental Consultants come with full DNS record control. Our Kabul engineers can configure your MX records, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC for Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 at zero additional setup charge.

How can I pay for my domain in Afghanistan?

We offer multiple convenient local payment methods: direct bank transfer to our corporate accounts at Kabul Bank or Azizi Bank in Afghanis (AFN) or US Dollars (USD), cash payment with formal statutory tax receipt at our Kabul head office (House 22, Street 17 Karte 3), or international wire transfer.

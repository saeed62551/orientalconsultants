Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

Oriental Consultants Afghanistan (“Oriental Consultants”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), registered with the Ministry of Commerce & Industry (License No. I-11011) and Ministry of Finance (TIN: 1045181011), is committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of corporate and individual clients, donors, and website visitors.

Our engagement protocols conform to the International Ethics Standards Board for Accountants (IESBA) Code of Ethics regarding professional confidentiality.

2. Information We Collect

In delivering professional statutory audit, tax advisory, ERP setup, and cloud services, we may collect and process:

  • Contact Information: Names, business email addresses, telephone numbers, and corporate titles submitted via contact forms, inquiries, or engagement letters.
  • Financial & Statutory Records: Trial balances, general ledgers, tax filings (BRT, wage withholding), audit working papers, and donor grant agreements provided exclusively for contractual execution.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Domain registration details, DNS configurations, and IP records required for IT infrastructure, Google Workspace, or Microsoft 365 licensing.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process information strictly for legitimate commercial and legal purposes, including:

  • Performing statutory audit and financial assurance under International Standards on Auditing (ISA).
  • Fulfilling Afghanistan Ministry of Finance (MoF) tax filing, BRT compliance, and clearance obligations.
  • Configuring QuikBok, Odoo ERP, and cloud hosting infrastructure requested by the client.
  • Complying with applicable laws, anti-money laundering regulations, and regulatory oversight in Kabul.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

Oriental Consultants does not sell, rent, or trade client data. Information is shared only under strict necessity with:

  • Regulatory Authorities: Afghan Ministry of Finance (MoF) or judicial bodies where explicitly mandated by statutory law.
  • Authorized Technology Partners: Software vendors (e.g., TSplus, Odoo, Google Workspace) strictly to license and provision requested services.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard physical, organizational, and technological safeguards to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure. Audit working papers and tax records are retained in accordance with statutory record-retention requirements under Afghan commercial and tax codes.

Comprehensive Donor Audit Solutions

Adhering strictly to International Standards on Auditing (ISA 800/805) and donor-specific financial guidelines.

UN Agency Project Audits

Verification of project financial reports (FACE forms), direct cash transfers, and implementing partner agreements for UNMAS, UNDP, UNICEF, UNHCR, and WFP in accordance with Harmonized Approach to Cash Transfers (HACT) frameworks.

  • ✓ HACT spot-checks and financial micro-assessments
  • ✓ Direct implementation cost verification & co-funding review
  • ✓ Physical assets & inventory distribution confirmation

USAID & US Federal Grant Audits

Audits conforming to the USAID Guidelines for Financial Audits Contracted by Foreign Recipients and OMB Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200). Led major engagements including the $248,000 USAID/AWDP program with 100% audit clearance.

  • ✓ Fund accountability statement & cost-share testing
  • ✓ US government Yellow Book / GAGAS compliance
  • ✓ Indirect cost rate calculation (NICRA) verification

World Bank & ADB Grant Reviews

Financial audit and expenditure verification of World Bank funded trust funds (ARTF), Asian Development Bank grants, and IFC projects. Executed the $355,000 World Bank/ASDP program without a single questioned cost.

  • ✓ Designated account (DA) reconciliations & SOE validation
  • ✓ Procurement regulation conformity (World Bank New Procurement Framework)
  • ✓ Covenant compliance & environmental/social milestone audit

INGO Statutory & MoEc Annual Audits

Comprehensive statutory financial audits for international and national NGOs registered under the Afghan Ministry of Economy (MoEc) NGO Law, fulfilling mandatory annual submission requirements.

  • ✓ Ministry of Economy semi-annual & annual reporting conformity
  • ✓ Administrative expense ratio analysis (conforming to legal limits)
  • ✓ NGO tax exemption certificates & withholding verification

Afghan Tax Law 2009 Reference

Statutory Afghan Tax Rates & Withholding Thresholds

Under the Ministry of Finance and Afghanistan Revenue Department (ARD) guidelines, organizations operating in Afghanistan must strictly adhere to the following statutory rates to avoid penalties:

Business Receipts Tax (BRT):
2%: Manufacturing, processing, industrial inputs.
4%: General commercial services, consulting, trade.
5%: Restaurants, hotels, hospitality services.
10%: Telecommunications and premium hospitality services.
Wage Withholding Tax (Article 46):
0% for monthly salary up to AFN 5,000.
2% on salary between AFN 5,001 and AFN 12,500.
10% on salary between AFN 12,501 and AFN 100,000.
20% on monthly salary exceeding AFN 100,000.
Rental Withholding Tax (Article 58):
10%: On monthly rent between AFN 10,000 and AFN 100,000.
15%: On monthly rent exceeding AFN 100,000.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Key considerations for businesses, NGOs, and foreign contractors engaging accounting services in Afghanistan.

How does Oriental Consultants handle multi-currency transactions (USD, AFN, EUR)?

We configure dual-currency or multi-currency ledgers in ERPs like KuickBok Enterprise or Odoo. Transactions are recorded in the transaction currency and translated to the functional currency (AFN) using the official Da Afghanistan Bank exchange rates on transaction dates, strictly adhering to IAS 21 for realized and unrealized FX gains or losses.

Can you help our organization clear back-taxes and penalties with the Ministry of Finance?

Yes. We provide tax audit defense, ledger reconciliation, and tax assessment representation before the Afghan Revenue Department (ARD). We assist organizations in reconstructing lost or incomplete accounts, calculating exact liabilities, and negotiating formal tax settlement plans.

Do you provide full outsourcing or can you support our in-house finance team?

We offer both models: (1) Complete Financial Outsourcing, where our accountants manage your books, payroll, and filings entirely; and (2) Co-Sourcing / Advisory, where we supervise your internal team, conduct monthly reconciliations, oversee year-end closes, and ensure tax compliance.

How do you guarantee confidentiality and data security?

We enforce strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), encrypted off-site cloud backups, role-based access control, and adhere to international standards on professional ethics and data privacy. Your financial records are secured with bank-grade encryption.

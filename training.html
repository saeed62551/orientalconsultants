Transformational Impact

We Don't Just Train. We Transform.

Traditional lecture-style training fails to produce sustainable institutional change. At Oriental Consultants, our training programs are structured on experiential learning models: Learn → Apply → Improve → Excel. We combine rigorous conceptual frameworks with real-world Afghan case studies, interactive simulations, and immediate workplace application.

Led by President Sayyed ul Abrar (Certified IFC Business Edge Trainer), our pedagogical methods are designed to engage adult learners, dismantle organizational friction, and instill actionable technical and managerial competencies.

Kirkpatrick 4-Level Evaluation: Every program is evaluated against Level 1 (Reaction), Level 2 (Pre/Post Learning Tests), Level 3 (Behavioral Changes on the Job), and Level 4 (Measurable Business Results).

Core Curriculum

Our 8 Core Capacity Building Disciplines

Standardized modules that can be delivered as turnkey courses or customized to your organization's specific training needs assessment (TNA).

Discipline 01

Leadership & Strategic Management

Designed for C-suite executives, project directors, and department heads. Focuses on strategic visioning, change management, navigating institutional crises, and high-performance team building.

  • ✓ Strategic planning frameworks and OKR/KPI setting
  • ✓ Change management in volatile Afghan environments
  • ✓ Emotional intelligence and conflict resolution
  • ✓ Delegation, accountability, and performance appraisals
Discipline 02

Finance & Tax for Non-Finance Managers

Demystifying financial statements for operational leaders, program managers, and entrepreneurs. Teaches participants how to interpret Balance Sheets, control budgets, and comply with Afghan Tax Law 2009.

  • ✓ Reading and interpreting P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow
  • ✓ Afghan BRT (2%, 4%, 5%, 10%) and salary withholding taxes
  • ✓ Budget formulation, burn-rate monitoring, and variance control
  • ✓ Fiduciary fraud detection and petty cash safeguards
Discipline 03

Good Governance & Board Oversight

Strengthening the fiduciary and strategic oversight of Boards of Directors, Board of Trustees, and Executive Committees in commercial banks, private corporations, and large NGOs.

  • ✓ Board fiduciary duties, liability, and independence
  • ✓ Establishing effective Audit and Risk Committees
  • ✓ Anti-corruption, whistleblowing, and conflict of interest codes
  • ✓ Transparent reporting to shareholders and regulatory authorities
Discipline 04

Project Management & PMBOK Standards

Rigorous project cycle management aligned with Project Management Institute (PMI) standards. Covers scope baseline, Work Breakdown Structures (WBS), Gantt scheduling, and donor deliverables tracking.

  • ✓ Project initiation, charter drafting, and stakeholder mapping
  • ✓ Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) and Critical Path Method (CPM)
  • ✓ Risk management matrices and mitigation strategies
  • ✓ Managing project close-out, asset disposal, and final donor reports
Discipline 05

M&E & Digital Data Analytics

Practical training for monitoring and evaluation (M&E) teams, field enumerators, and program officers. Teaches indicator formulation, mobile survey deployment, and quantitative data analysis.

  • ✓ Developing SMART indicators and Results Frameworks
  • ✓ Mobile data collection setup using KoboToolbox and ODK
  • ✓ Data cleaning, validation, and analysis in Excel and SPSS
  • ✓ Evidence-based reporting for donors and executive stakeholders
Discipline 06

Procurement & Supply Chain Management

Comprehensive instruction on transparent procurement procedures adhering to World Bank Procurement Regulations, USAID guidelines, and Afghan National Procurement Authority (NPA) benchmarks.

  • ✓ Drafting RFPs, RFQs, and bidding documents
  • ✓ Bid evaluation committee procedures and scoring matrices
  • ✓ Contract administration and vendor dispute management
  • ✓ Multi-warehouse inventory tracking and stock reconciliation
Discipline 07

Digital ERP & Software Masterclasses

Hands-on software labs for finance and operations personnel on QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise 2024 and Odoo ERP. Led by certified ProAdvisors and Odoo functional consultants.

  • ✓ QuickBooks multi-user navigation, chart of accounts, and invoicing
  • ✓ Odoo Accounting, Inventory, Purchase, and Sales workflows
  • ✓ Bank reconciliation and custom Afghan tax report extraction
  • ✓ User permissions, audit trails, and automated backups
Discipline 08

Workplace Ethics, Safeguarding & Gender

Crucial institutional policies for organizations operating in Afghanistan. Covers Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA), child safeguarding, workplace ethics, and inclusive employment.

  • ✓ PSEA and international safeguarding standards
  • ✓ Establishing confidential grievance and reporting mechanisms
  • ✓ Inclusive workspace policies conforming to local legal contexts
  • ✓ Cultural sensitivity and community stakeholder engagement

Program Details

Frequently Asked Questions (Training FAQ)

Key information regarding custom curricula, participant certification, and scheduling.

Can Oriental Consultants design a bespoke curriculum for our donor-funded project?

Yes. We regularly conduct formal Training Needs Assessments (TNA) for donor projects (World Bank, USAID, UN agencies). We formulate customized learning objectives, participant workbooks, Dari/Pashto slide decks, pre/post-tests, and comprehensive post-training impact reports conforming to donor reporting requirements.

Are participants awarded recognized certificates?

Yes. All participants who complete the required attendance and pass the post-training examination receive an official Certificate of Completion issued by Oriental Consultants (accredited under MoCI License I-11011 and linked with the Grow Learn Connect / World Bank-IFC heritage).

Do you deliver training outside of Kabul?

Yes. Our mobile trainer network covers all 34 provinces of Afghanistan. We have conducted extensive training programs in provincial capitals such as Herat, Mazar-i-Sharif, Kandahar, Jalalabad, Bamyan, and Kunduz, respecting all local security and cultural considerations.

What is the typical duration of a capacity building workshop?

Depending on the depth of the subject matter, courses range from intensive 1-day executive masterclasses to 3-day operational workshops and 2-week comprehensive ERP/Financial certification programs.

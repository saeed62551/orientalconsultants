Why Audit Quality is Fundamental in Afghanistan

When there is a need for audit and compliance tasks, it is absolutely vital that you choose an auditor you can trust. Audit quality is of fundamental importance for maintaining trust in the financial reporting process and the integrity of financial information. That is why our Audit & Assurance team members are continually striving to ensure that audits are carried out to the highest acceptable standards.

We provide audit services to a wide range of clients across Afghanistan. Methodological Rigor

The 6-Phase Audit Execution Framework

How Oriental Consultants delivers independent, ISA-compliant audits from pre-engagement through final opinion issuance.

Phase Stage Name Auditing Standards & Scope Key Deliverables
Phase 1 Engagement & Acceptance Independence verification, client acceptance procedures under ISQC 1 / ISQM 1, and formalization of audit scope and terms. Audit Engagement Letter (ISA 210), Independence Declarations.
Phase 2 Risk Assessment & Materiality Understanding the entity and its environment (ISA 315), calculating overall and performance materiality (ISA 320), and identifying fraud risks (ISA 240). Audit Strategy Memorandum, Materiality Threshold Calculation Sheet.
Phase 3 Internal Controls Evaluation Walkthroughs of key accounting cycles (Procurement, Payroll, Revenue, Cash). Testing design and operating effectiveness of controls. Internal Control Walkthrough Files, Test of Controls (TOC) Working Papers.
Phase 4 Substantive Testing & Verification Analytical procedures, detailed transaction testing, third-party confirmations (banks, legal counsel, debtors), and physical asset/inventory inspections. Substantive Audit Worksheets, Third-Party Confirmation Summaries.
Phase 5 Draft Findings & Management Letter Synthesizing identified control deficiencies, misstatements, and non-compliance. Formulating root causes and actionable recommendations. Draft Management Letter, Internal Control Deficiency Matrix.
Phase 6 Opinion Issuance & Exit Conference Exit conference with board / management, obtaining Management Representation Letter (ISA 580), and issuing final certified Independent Auditor's Report. Independent Auditor's Report (ISA 700), Final Signed Management Letter.

Frequently Asked Questions (Audit FAQ)

Essential information on Afghan audit regulations, ISA compliance, and engagement timelines.

Is an annual external audit mandatory for companies registered in Afghanistan?

Yes. Under Afghanistan Commercial Law and Ministry of Commerce & Industries (MoCI) guidelines, all registered corporations, limited liability companies (LLCs), and non-governmental organizations (NGOs) are required to submit an audited annual financial statement along with their tax returns to renew their commercial license and maintain active corporate standing.

How long does a standard statutory audit take from start to finish?

A standard statutory audit for small to medium-sized enterprises typically takes 2 to 3 weeks once all PBC (Prepared by Client) documents and reconciliations are made available. For large organizations with multi-provincial branches or complex donor programs, the process typically takes 4 to 6 weeks.

Can Oriental Consultants audit provincial sites outside Kabul?

Yes. We maintain active operational presence and experienced audit teams across all 34 provinces of Afghanistan (including Herat, Mazar-i-Sharif, Kandahar, Jalalabad, and Kunduz), enabling us to perform on-site inventory counts, cash counts, and physical asset verifications safely and efficiently.

What is the difference between an Unmodified and a Qualified audit opinion?

An Unmodified Opinion (Clean Report) indicates that the financial statements present fairly, in all material respects, the financial position and performance of the entity in accordance with applicable financial reporting frameworks (IFRS). A Qualified Opinion is issued when the auditor concludes that misstatements, individually or in aggregate, are material but not pervasive to the financial statements, or when sufficient appropriate evidence could not be obtained for a specific area.

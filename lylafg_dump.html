02 Who we are

One partner, from tender to installation.

“Capacity, credibility, dependability and reliability.”

Lutfullah Yasir Ltd (LYL) is a logistics service provider working across trading, supply and installation of medical machinery, laboratory reagents and consumables, laboratory equipment, and livestock and dairy processing lines.

The company has commissioned and installed machinery across Afghanistan, supported by a team of procurement, logistics, technical and training specialists. It is the largest supplier of medical equipment to Ministry of Public Health hospitals, according to its company profile, and one of the leading companies in the dairy-processing sector — with milk collection centres and quality-assurance laboratories established in several provinces.

LYL is the authorised dealer of NIPRO Japan and NIPRO Singapore, whose nephrology products are registered with the FDA and in Europe, alongside eight further manufacturers of catheters, orthopaedic and physiotherapy equipment, blood tubing, angiography products, incinerators and agricultural machinery.

Since 2008 the company has delivered for the Ministry of Public Health, the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock, UN agencies, USAID-funded programmes and NGOs.

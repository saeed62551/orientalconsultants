Wafa Consultants • Associate Consulting Partner

Oriental Consultants is proud to collaborate with Wafa Consultants as our premier associate consulting partner. Combining Oriental Consultants' financial audit, ERP, and institutional governance expertise with Wafa Consultants' extensive engineering oversight, environmental impact assessments (ESIA), and nationwide provincial field research networks.

Associate Capability Profile

Wafa Consultants Core Expertise

  • Engineering Quality Assurance & Oversight: Structural inspections, bill of quantities (BOQ) verification, and infrastructure compliance for civil works.
  • Environmental & Social Impact (ESIA): World Bank and ADB standard environmental screening, social risk mitigation, and resettlement action plans.
  • Provincial Community Access & Surveys: Established community liaison networks across all 34 provinces of Afghanistan enabling safe, reliable field data collection.
  • Socio-Economic Feasibility Studies: Quantitative baseline assessments, household surveys, market appraisals, and agricultural value-chain analyses.
